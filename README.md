# MaestroAI

An AI analyst that watches and forecasts La Liga.

## What it does
- **Predicts:** posts win, draw, and loss probabilities before every La Liga match
- **Watches:** reviews match highlights and writes up what happened and why
- **Keeps score:** grades every prediction against the betting market, in public

In progress. Building in public and writing about it on Medium.
